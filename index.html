Abstract

Contrastive inter-modal representation learning is powerful in zero-shot classification, text-queried audio retrieval, and audio-queried text retrieval. Recently, image-text contrastive learning has been proposed with soft labels generated from text-text similarities between all samples in a batch. As our first contribution, we use such intra-modal text-text similarities as inter-modal text-audio similarity targets. Second, we generalize this to also gain information from audio-audio similarities. Third, we propose a method to obtain similarity targets from these intra-modal similarities using uncertainty estimation. We further investigate additionally employing a modality classification branch and using MSE-guided estimation. Finally, in a specified training regime, we show that our methods are competitive with SOTA on a number of downstream tasks, while using only a fraction of training data and batch size. Our utilized training data and pre-trained models are publicly available.
